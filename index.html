What is ABG Elite Skills?

ABG Elite Skills is an Edmonton hockey development program run by current NCAA Division I and professional hockey players. We run summer camps with a 6:1 skater-to-coach ratio, video breakdowns and mentorship calls, and private sessions in spring and summer.

When are camps held?

Camps run in the summer only. Every ABG coach is a current NCAA or pro player, so from fall through winter we're away playing our own seasons. Summer 2027 dates and early registration go to our email list first. Most camps run at KC Twin Arenas, 13160 140 Avenue NW, in north Edmonton.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes. Private, semi-private, and team sessions run in the spring and summer, when our coaches are home in Edmonton. Email abgeliteskills@gmail.com for rates and availability.

What is the D1 Blueprint?

A free guide by Logan Acheson on what matters for player development at every age from 7 to 18, from building focus early to handling the jump to junior. Enter your email above and we'll send it to you.
